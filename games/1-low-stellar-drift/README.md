# STELLAR DRIFT - a No Man's Sky-style space exploration game (Godot 4.7.2)

> **Как запустить готовую сборку / How to run the ready-made build:** см. / see [HOW_TO_PLAY.md](../../HOW_TO_PLAY.md) в корне репозитория / in the repository root. Если ниже упоминаются другие файлы запуска — их в этом репозитории нет / if other launch files are mentioned below, they are not part of this repository.

## Run
Open the folder in Godot 4.7.2 and press Play (main scene `res://scenes/main.tscn`), or:
`Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64.exe --path "<this folder>"`. Keyboard + mouse. No extra installs.
Optional: `-- --autotest --shotdir=res://shots` runs a scripted tour that saves screenshots (used for my testing).

## Controls
Ship: W/S thrust, A/D strafe, R/Ctrl up/down, mouse pitch+yaw, Q/E roll, Shift boost, hold Space = pulse drive, V cockpit/chase camera.
Landed: W/Space/R take off, F exit ship, mouse orbits the camera.
On foot: WASD walk, Shift sprint, Space jump (hold in air = jetpack), E collect, T scan, F board ship.
General: M galaxy map (click star, Enter or button to warp), Esc pause. A controls screen is in the main/pause menus.

## What was built
- **Galaxy**: seeded spiral of 90 stars (`gal.gd`), procedural sky shader (stars, galactic band, nebula), galaxy map with a live schematic of the current system. Warping from the map costs 3 resource units (you start with 6; collect crystals on planets).
- **Star system "Aurelia"** (G star, glowing shader sphere + corona/flare) with 7 bodies: Eden (lush), Dunmar (desert), Glacia (frozen), Jovan (gas giant with rings, not landable), Ignis (volcanic), Miasma (toxic), Selene (barren moon orbiting the gas giant). Six are landable, five distinct atmospheric biomes + an airless moon. Planets orbit (speed-up when far from planets, frozen near them so landed players are stable); clouds spin. Second system **Cygnaris Prime** (blue B star, different planet order); every other star is generated by seed too.
- **Planets**: spherical cube-sphere terrain from seeded noise, per-biome terrain shape (hills, dunes, ridged spikes, terraces, craters, rough), palette, sky/fog colours, gravity (3-12 m/s2), hazards (heat/cold/toxic/vacuum draining the exosuit), weather particles (rain, dust, snow, ash, spores), oceans (water/lava/acid), fauna (floating jellies), flora/rocks/crystals scattered by seed with MultiMeshes, a landing pad + outpost + beacon per planet, collectable resource crystals. Day/night cycle (the star light and sky rotate around the planet, 7 min/day), atmosphere glow shell, altitude-dependent sky/fog.
- **Seamless flight**: space -> atmosphere -> landing -> on foot -> take off, no loading screens. Speed limit scales with altitude (so pulse drive slows you automatically near planets), entry heat (screen glow + flame particles), landing assist, automatic gear, hard-landing damage. Floating origin keeps coordinates near zero (no jitter at 60,000 m scales). A high-detail terrain patch follows the player on a worker thread; the coarse mesh sinks under it to avoid z-fighting.
- **Black hole**: dark event horizon, animated accretion disc shader and a screen-space gravitational-lensing shader (Einstein ring, photon ring, warped background). Mechanics: gravity grows with 1/d^2 from 30 km; HUD warnings at 30 km (anomaly), 12 km (pulse drive disabled), 4.2 km (PONR countdown); point of no return at 2.8 km where pull exceeds the ship's boost thrust (engines cannot escape); camera shake grows; crossing the horizon (500 m) triggers a warp-tunnel transition and teleports you to the black hole's partner star system (Aurelia <-> Cygnaris Prime), like NMS black holes moving you across the galaxy.
- **Models (all made through Blender MCP, port 9877, `low_effort.blend`)**: ship, astronaut, tree, cactus, mushroom, rock, crystal, landing pad, outpost, jelly - exported as GLB to `assets/models/` (script: `scripts/model.py`).
- **UI**: main menu, HUD (location, altitude, gravity, hazard, speed + limit, suit/jetpack/hull, context hints, world markers for planets/star/black hole/pad/ship), pause menu, controls screen, galaxy map.

## Known limitations
- No collision with trees/structures; no audio; no combat/economy. Fauna is decorative.
- Planets are small (~500-760 m radius) and orbit distances compressed so trips take seconds/minutes.
- Orbital motion is only noticeable while cruising far from planets (and in the map schematic).
- Ocean has no swimming (you wade slowly); the moon of the gas giant moves relative to the sky because orbits are time-scaled.
- Ambiguity decisions: units are metres, planets/orbits are scaled-down; black hole destination is fixed (Aurelia <-> Cygnaris Prime).
